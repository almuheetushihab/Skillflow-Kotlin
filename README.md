# SkillFlow - Micro-Learning for Career Growth

**SkillFlow** is a modern Android application designed for busy professionals and students who want to advance their careers through micro-learning. Instead of overwhelming courses, SkillFlow provides three "Knowledge Nuggets" every day to help you stay sharp and reach your goals.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Daily Knowledge Nuggets:** Get 3 interactive flashcards or short articles every day tailored to your career path.
- **Career Selection:** Choose your focus area (e.g., Android Developer, Backend Dev, UI/UX Designer) to receive relevant content.
- **Daily Streak & Progress:** Stay motivated with a streak counter and track your daily task completion.
- **Interactive Flashcards:** Flip cards to reveal detailed information and mark them as done once you've learned them.
- **Career Roadmap:** Visualize your learning journey with a step-by-step graphical roadmap.
- **Reinforcement Quizzes:** Test your knowledge with quick MCQs at the end of each day.
- **Offline Access:** Save your favorite nuggets to bookmarks and read them anytime, even without an internet connection.
- **Smart Notifications:** Receive daily reminders to complete your learning goals.

## 🛠️ Technical Stack

- **UI:** [Jetpack Compose](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/compose) with Material 3 design system.
- **Architecture:** MVVM + Clean Architecture (Data, Domain, Presentation).
- **Dependency Injection:** [Hilt](https://dagger.dev/hilt/).
- **Networking:** [Retrofit](https://square.github.io/retrofit/) + OkHttp + Kotlinx Serialization.
- **Database:** [Room Persistence Library](https://developer.android.com/training/data-storage/room).
- **Image Loading:** [Coil](https://coil-kt.github.io/coil/).
- **Animations:** [Lottie](https://airbnb.design/lottie/) for engaging UI interactions.
- **Background Tasks:** [WorkManager](https://developer.android.com/topic/libraries/architecture/workmanager) for notifications.
- **Async Processing:** Kotlin Coroutines & Flow.

## 📱 Screen Breakdown

1. **Onboarding:** Career goal selection and welcome screen.
2. **Dashboard:** Daily tasks, progress bar, and streak counter.
3. **Flashcard Details:** Flip-to-reveal content with swipable pager.
4. **Roadmap:** Graphical step-by-step career guide.
5. **Quiz:** MCQ interface for learning reinforcement.
6. **Bookmarks:** List of saved topics for offline reading.
7. **Profile:** Settings for goals, notifications, and dark mode.
